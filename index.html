Tiny money stories

Aarav and the Bicycle Jar

Aarav wanted a red cycle that cost ₹4,500. He fed Save every Sunday until the jar clinked when he walked. In March, it clinked all the way to the shop.

What did they learn?

A goal with a price tag is easier to save for than a wish.

Meher’s Diwali Share

Meher had ₹60 in Share. She bought diyas for the watchman uncle’s little room. Her pocket felt lighter. Her chest felt warmer.

What did they learn?

Giving feels good, and it costs less than you think.

Kabir’s Oops Day

Kabir spent every coin on one giant ice-cream and had none left for a cricket ball. Now he splits first, licks later.

What did they learn?

Split first, spend later, and the cricket ball is always there.
